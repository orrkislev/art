Expanding regions strive to maximize their reach, stretching out like vibrant brushstrokes on a canvas. Colliding blobs engage in a dynamic dance, jostling for dominance until a delicate equilibrium is disrupted, and a state of entropy is reluctantly embraced.
This chaotic spectacle draws inspiration from the eclectic realms of Memphis design, the intricate tapestry of world maps, the cozy complexity of quilts, and the whimsical chaos of the multiverse seen in Rick and Morty. Each element contributes its own unique flavor, blending together in a harmonious clash of patterns and narratives.
